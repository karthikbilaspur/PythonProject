Adventure Game - ENHANCED (3 Levels)
Run: python adventure_game_enhanced.py
No extra libraries needed.

LEVEL 1 - The Two Doors (your original)
 - Red door: dragon sneak / fight
 - Blue door: fairy potion
 - Secret room: glowing amulet

LEVEL 2 - NEW - The Crystal Bridge
 - Riddle Keeper: 1 random riddle, 2 tries
 - Pick 1 of 3 chests, only 1 is safe (random each game)
 - Healing, damage, Crystal Key reward

LEVEL 3 - NEW - The Final Guardian
 - Turn-based boss fight, Guardian has 60 HP
 - 1 Attack / 2 Defend / 3 Use item
 - Your items from Level 1 + 2 actually matter here:
   Dragon Sword +10 damage, Glowing Amulet +10 damage,
   Fairy Potion / Healing Crystal heal you, Fairy Blessing blocks a hit
 - Bonus +100 if you finish with 70+ health
 - Rank at the end based on final score

New systems: Health (100), Inventory, Score shown after every action.
